Tally is a desk for private loans between two people on **Midnight**. Midnight is a blockchain built so some facts can stay private.

Here is the idea in plain words. Two people make a loan. The **amount** (how much money) and the **due** date stay private. The public chain only sees that a loan exists and what step it is on. When the loan is fully done, either person can prove they have a repaid history. A checker only learns **yes** or **no**. They do not learn how much was lent.

## The path every loan follows

**Offer → Accept → Send funds → Repay → Settle → Prove good record**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Offer">The lender writes the amount and due in private. On the live network they name the borrower with a private ID.</Step>
  <Step title="Accept">The borrower agrees.</Step>
  <Step title="Send funds">The lender records that money was sent. The chain stores a sealed mark called a payment commitment. It still does not show the amount.</Step>
  <Step title="Repay">The borrower marks the loan as paid back.</Step>
  <Step title="Settle">Either person closes the loan. Tally adds them to an allowlist. An allowlist is like a guest list of people who finished a repaid loan.</Step>
  <Step title="Prove good record">Someone proves they are on that guest list. A checker only sees Pass or Fail.</Step>
</Steps>

**Preprod** means Midnight’s public test network. Tokens there are for testing. They are not real money.

## Try it in 5 minutes

1. Open [the desk](https://www.tallylend.site). A first visit opens on Local desk, with no wallet.
2. To use Preprod, install Lace or 1AM, set the wallet to Preprod, and request free tNIGHT from the [Midnight Preprod faucet](https://faucet.preprod.midnight.network/). Generate tDUST in the wallet.
3. Click **Connect wallet**, switch to **Preprod**, and make an offer with the other person's private ID.

## What Tally does not try to be

- A credit score company
- A place that shows loan amounts to the world
- A marketplace of many open loans
- Mainnet (the live money network) yet

Tally answers one question. Are you on the repaid allowlist. Yes or no.
