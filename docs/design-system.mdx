title: Design system
description: Warm ledger desk. Fraunces, Figtree, oxblood.

Tally should feel like an old trade ledger, not a neon crypto casino. Long ago, people split a wooden stick to record a debt. Each person kept half. The wood had to match. Tally borrows that idea. The public chain holds the matching grain. Amount and names stay in the private halves.

Product name: **Tally**. Logo: the split stick in `brand/tally-stick.svg`. Desk headline: **The other half stays private.**

## Colors

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Background | `#1C1914` | Page (not pure black) |
| Surface | `#2A241C` | Cards and loan paper |
| Bone | `#E8DCC8` | Headlines |
| Ink | `#C4B49A` | Body text |
| Muted | `#8A7A64` | Small labels |
| Oxblood | `#8C2F2B` | Main buttons and the Funded seal (use sparingly) |
| Moss | `#4F6F54` | Settled seal |
| Rule | `#3D3428` | Thin borders |

## Type

- Headlines: **Fraunces**
- Body and forms: **Figtree**
- Small labels: Figtree, wider letter spacing, uppercase

Avoid Inter, Roboto, and Space Grotesk.

## Buttons

Primary buttons use oxblood fill. On hover they shrink a little (`scale(0.97)`). They never grow.

## What we refuse

No gradient text. No purple neon. No glass blur stacks. No pure white or pure black. No exclamation marks. No words like seamless, robust, empower, unlock, or supercharge. No three-column feature grids. The product is a desk of loans, not a chart dashboard.
