## What you will see

1. Open the desk
2. Use Lace or 1AM on Preprod, or switch to **Local desk**
3. Lender makes an **Offer** (amount stays off-ledger)
4. Borrower **Accepts**
5. Lender **Disburses** (Funded seal)
6. Borrower **Repays**, then either person **Settles**
7. **Prove standing**, then Verifier → **Verify access** → **Pass**

The instrument row keeps saying **Amount: off-ledger** the whole time. Off-ledger means the public chain does not show how much money was in the loan.
