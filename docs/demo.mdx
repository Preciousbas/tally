## What you will see

1. Open the desk. A first visit opens on **Local desk**
2. Use Lace or 1AM on Preprod, or stay on Local desk
3. Lender makes an **Offer** (amount stays Hidden)
4. Borrower **Accepts**
5. Lender clicks **Send funds** (Funded seal)
6. Borrower **Repays**, then either person **Settles**
7. **Prove good record**, then **Check a record** → **Check record** → **Pass**

The loan row keeps saying **Amount: Hidden** the whole time. Hidden means the public chain does not show how much money was in the loan.
