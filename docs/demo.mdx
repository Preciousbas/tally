## What it shows

1. Open [https://tally-jet-mu.vercel.app](https://tally-jet-mu.vercel.app)
2. Lace or 1AM on Preprod, or **Local desk**
3. Lender: Offer (amount stays off-ledger)
4. Borrower: Accept
5. Lender: Disburse (Funded seal; chain never shows how much)
6. Borrower: Repay → Settle
7. Prove standing → Verifier → **Verify access** → **Pass**

The instrument row stays **Amount: off-ledger** through the whole path.
