---
title: Privacy
description: Amount stays private. A checker only learns yes or no.
---

Privacy is the point of Tally. An explorer can see that loans move through seals. An explorer cannot see how much money was in a loan.

## What someone looking at the chain can see

- That a loan id exists
- The **status** seal (Offered, Accepted, Funded, Repaid, Settled)
- Two **private ID** codes for lender and borrower (long public codes, not real names). The contract calls each one a Desk ID
- That a **payment commitment** was set when funds were sent (a sealed mark, not the amount)
- The **allowlist** Merkle root (a fingerprint of the guest list of settled relationships)
- That a **nullifier** was used at Settle (a one-time ticket so the same settle cannot be reused)

## What they cannot see

- The loan **amount**
- The **due** date
- Your browser **secret** (the value that produces your private ID)
- Which allowlist leaf you used when you prove a good record
- The money terms of older loans

## What someone checking a record learns

On the desk this role is **Check a record**. They open technical details if they need the allowlist root, then click **Check record**.

They learn one answer. Pass or Fail. That means “is this prover on the settled-relationship allowlist for this contract.”

They do not learn amount, due, leaf index, or who the other party was.

## What a repaid-count check learns

**Prove loans repaid** is a separate proof. The borrower shows they repaid at least N loans, where N is 1, 2, or 3. A checker learns that number and Pass or Fail. They do not learn which loans, the amounts, the due dates, or the lenders from this proof. The proof also does not put the borrower’s private ID on the transcript.

Loan rows are public and already show the borrower private ID and status. Someone who already knows that ID can count that person’s settled loans by reading the book. This circuit does not hide that.

The leaf format is unchanged. The public Preprod contract includes this circuit. That book started empty. Settled loans on the previous contract stay there.

## Private facts vs public facts

Private facts live in **witnesses**. Witnesses are values the circuit checks inside a proof. The public ledger never prints them as clear text.

Public facts live in `LoanPublic` rows and in the allowlist and nullifier sets. Those are what indexers and explorers can read.

Loan rows on the desk always say **Amount: Hidden**. Hidden means “kept off the public book.”
