Source file: [`contract/tally.compact`](https://github.com/Preciousbas/tally/blob/main/contract/tally.compact). Compile with Compact `0.31.1`. The desk talks to the contract through `TallyAPI` in the `api` package.

A **ledger** is the on-chain book the contract keeps. A **circuit** is one action you can call.

## Public book

The contract stores:

- A map of loans
- The next loan id
- An allowlist tree of settled relationships
- A set of nullifiers (one-time settle tickets)

Each public loan row holds status, lender Desk ID, borrower Desk ID, and a payment commitment. The desk calls a Desk ID a private ID. There is no amount field on that row. On screen the amount reads Hidden.

## Private witnesses

Witnesses are private inputs. They include the browser secret, amount, due, and the standing proof path. The contract also mixes in a fixed public number, `1234`, when it builds a Desk ID. That number is a domain tag, not a password anyone types. Circuits read these inputs. Explorers do not print the secret, the amount, or the due date as clear text.

## The circuits

### offerLoan

Lender creates a loan aimed at a borrower Desk ID. Amount and due must be greater than zero. You cannot offer to yourself.

### acceptLoan

Borrower accepts an Offered loan. Only the named Desk ID works.

### disburse

The desk button is **Send funds**. Lender attaches a non-zero payment commitment after Accept. Seal becomes Funded. Amount is still not a public argument.

### repay

Borrower marks a Funded loan as Repaid.

### settle

Lender or borrower closes a Repaid loan. Inserts a nullifier and a relationship leaf. Seal becomes Settled. A second settle on the same ticket fails.

### proveStanding

The desk button is **Prove good record**. Builds a membership proof against the allowlist. No public amount. Failures include “not on allowlist” style errors when the leaf or root is wrong.

### proveRepaidCount

The desk button is **Prove loans repaid**. The borrower proves they repaid at least N loans. N is a public number from 1 to 3. The proof writes nothing new on the ledger. It does not reveal which loans, the lenders, or the borrower’s Desk ID.

Each counted leaf is the same repaid relationship leaf Settle already inserts (`tally:rel:v1:repaid`). The leaf is rebuilt with the prover as the borrower. A person who only lent cannot pass. Slots past N repeat the first loan, so one loan cannot be counted twice.

This circuit is on the public Preprod contract `635a4817b625b7954f52b71180da0dbf6c1a50bb538824a3c51fc514e11e77b8`. That deploy started an empty allowlist. Settled loans on the previous contract stay there. They do not move into this tree. A count proof passes only after loans are settled here.

Loan rows are still public. They already show a borrower Desk ID and a status. Someone who already knows a Desk ID can count that person’s settled loans by reading the book. This circuit does not hide that. It lets the borrower prove “at least N” without pointing at the loans, and without putting their Desk ID on this proof.

## Tests

`npm test` in the contract workspace checks identity, the full loan path, nullifiers, standing pass/fail cases, and repaid-count pass/fail cases.
