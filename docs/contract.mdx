Source file: [`contract/tally.compact`](https://github.com/Preciousbas/tally/blob/main/contract/tally.compact). Compile with Compact `0.31.1`. The desk talks to the contract through `TallyAPI` in the `api` package.

A **ledger** is the on-chain book the contract keeps. A **circuit** is one action you can call.

## Public book

The contract stores:

- A map of loans
- The next loan id
- An allowlist tree of settled relationships
- A set of nullifiers (one-time settle tickets)

Each public loan row holds status, lender Desk ID, borrower Desk ID, and a payment commitment. There is no amount field on that row.

## Private witnesses

Witnesses are private inputs. They include user secret, PIN, amount, due, and the standing proof path pieces. Circuits read them. Explorers do not print them as clear text.

## The six circuits

### offerLoan

Lender creates a loan aimed at a borrower Desk ID. Amount and due must be greater than zero. You cannot offer to yourself.

### acceptLoan

Borrower accepts an Offered loan. Only the named Desk ID works.

### disburse

Lender attaches a non-zero payment commitment after Accept. Seal becomes Funded. Amount is still not a public argument.

### repay

Borrower marks a Funded loan as Repaid.

### settle

Lender or borrower closes a Repaid loan. Inserts a nullifier and a relationship leaf. Seal becomes Settled. A second settle on the same ticket fails.

### proveStanding

Builds a membership proof against the allowlist. No public amount. Failures include “not on allowlist” style errors when the leaf or root is wrong.

## Tests

`npm test` in the contract workspace checks identity, the full loan path, nullifiers, and standing pass/fail cases.
