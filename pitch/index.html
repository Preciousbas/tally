02 · Problem

Bureaus publish one score.

Trade credit was always pairwise. A split stick. Two halves. The grain had to match. Tally puts that instrument on Midnight.

03 · Metaphor

Public chain holds the grain.

Amount, due, and names stay in witnesses. The ledger stores status, a payment commitment, a Merkle leaf, and a nullifier.

04 · Dual ledger

What discloses, what does not.

Chain sees: id, status, payment grain, nullifier. You see: amount, due, counterparty. Identity is secret + PIN, not ownPublicKey.

05 · Demo

Offer, accept, disburse, repay, settle.

Disburse binds a real Preprod payment grain. Funded is an oxblood seal. The explorer cannot print the amount.

06 · Wave 2
