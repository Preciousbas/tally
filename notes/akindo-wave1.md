Wave 1 originated a private bilateral loan on Midnight. Terms and identities stay in witnesses. The ledger stores status, a payment grain, a Merkle root, and nullifiers. Disbursement binds a Preprod payment so judges can see a funded loan, not a mock amount on-chain. A settled loan inserts a relationship leaf for Wave 2 standing proofs. Not in this Wave: third-party score proofs, web2 attestors, a credit marketplace.
